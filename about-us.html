About Us

James Douglas brings a fresh, agile approach to mid- and senior-level recruitment, while drawing on the reputation, resources, and international network of EMA Partners. More than a search firm, James Douglas is a leadership advisory movement — built on EMA Partners’ legacy in India and renewed with a sharper purpose: creating value through people.

We believe that people shape organizations, and organizations shape society. Our role is to stand at the intersection of ambition and impact, partnering with leaders and organizations at moments that define the future.

At James Douglas, our values are not words. They are commitments.

Our Mission
To empower organizations and leaders through trusted advisory, meaningful partnerships, and people who make a difference.

To redefine leadership hiring by shaping teams and careers that move industries and communities forward.

Our Belief
