Are You Ready to Build, Lead, and Create Impact?

At James Douglas (an EMA Partners firm), we’re not just filling roles, we’re building long-term leadership partnerships across industries. And we’re looking for people who want to build something meaningful with us.

The professionals who thrive here are those who see recruitment as more than transactions. They are relationship builders, trusted advisors, and growth partners. They’re driven by impact, ownership, and the ambition to shape both client outcomes and their own careers.

We work with high-growth organizations and global leaders to identify and nurture exceptional talent — and we hold ourselves to the same standard. At James Douglas, your journey isn’t defined by a title; it’s defined by the opportunities you create, the relationships you build, and the influence you develop.

What It’s Like to Work
at James Douglas

At James Douglas, our work is centred on people. Not just roles, processes, or outcomes. We spend our days understanding what helps individuals and organisations succeed together, especially during moments that matter. That approach starts internally. We believe good advisory work comes from people who are thoughtful, curious, and willing to take responsibility for their craft. Our teams are encouraged to ask better questions, develop judgment over time, and engage with clients and candidates with honesty and respect.

We’ve shaped our culture to support learning, collaboration, and long-term growth. There’s space here to build depth, form strong professional relationships, and do work that feels considered rather than rushed. When our people grow, the quality of our work improves and that’s what ultimately allows us to serve our clients better.
